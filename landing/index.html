Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Business

From scheduling to payments, Bizzy handles it all so you can spend more time doing what you love.

Smart Scheduling

Drag-and-drop calendar, recurring jobs, crew assignments, and automatic reminders keep your schedule running smoothly.

Invoicing & Estimates

Create professional invoices and estimates in seconds. Accept payments, track overdue accounts, and get paid faster.

Time Tracking

Simple clock in/out, GPS verification, break tracking, and detailed timesheets for accurate payroll.

Client Messaging

Send appointment reminders, job updates, and follow-ups via SMS or email with customizable templates.

Financial Tracking

Track income, expenses, and profit in real-time. Scan receipts, categorize transactions, and export for tax season.

Team Management

Add crew members, assign roles, track locations, and manage permissions all from one dashboard.

Route Optimization

Automatically optimize your daily job stops to save time and fuel. Get turn-by-turn directions and reduce drive time between clients.
